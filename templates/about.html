About EcoRoute

Travel connects people, cultures, and economies — but it also contributes significantly to global carbon emissions, environmental degradation, and overtourism. Many travelers unknowingly increase their environmental footprint simply due to lack of accessible and understandable sustainability data.

EcoRoute is a technology-driven sustainability travel assistant designed to promote environmentally responsible mobility. The platform empowers users to make informed travel decisions by providing real-time insights into carbon impact, eco-friendly alternatives, and sustainability performance metrics.

What EcoRoute Offers

  • 🌍 Carbon Footprint Estimation for different transport modes
  • 🚆 Eco-Optimized Route Suggestions
  • ⚡ Electric Vehicle & Bicycle Accessibility Insights
  • 📊 Personalized Sustainability Scoring System
  • 🔔 Environmental Alerts & Eco Awareness Tools

By integrating environmental science principles with modern web technologies, EcoRoute bridges the gap between travel convenience and ecological responsibility. The platform encourages behavioral change through awareness, measurable impact, and intelligent recommendations.

Our Vision

We envision a future where sustainable travel is not an alternative — but the default standard. EcoRoute aims to demonstrate how digital innovation can actively contribute to climate action, responsible tourism, and long-term environmental resilience.

Project Background

Developed as a Bachelor of Technology (Computer Science) project, EcoRoute showcases how software engineering, data analysis, and environmental awareness can combine to address real-world challenges. This project reflects the belief that technology should not only solve problems — but also create positive societal impact.
