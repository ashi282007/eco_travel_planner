Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is EcoRoute?

EcoRoute is a sustainability-focused web application designed to promote environmentally responsible travel decisions. It provides carbon footprint insights, eco-friendly transport suggestions, and sustainability scoring to help users reduce their travel-related environmental impact.

2. What problem does EcoRoute aim to solve?

Modern travel contributes significantly to carbon emissions and environmental degradation. Many travelers are unaware of the environmental impact of their choices. EcoRoute addresses this gap by providing clear, accessible, and data-driven sustainability insights.

3. How does EcoRoute calculate carbon footprint?

The platform uses emission factor datasets for different modes of transport such as cars, trains, and flights. Based on distance and transport type, the system estimates approximate CO₂ emissions using standardized environmental calculation models.

4. What technologies were used to build EcoRoute?

EcoRoute is built using Python (Flask framework) for backend development, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript for frontend design, and integrated datasets for sustainability calculations. The architecture follows a modular and scalable web development approach.

5. Is the data used in EcoRoute reliable?

The carbon emission estimations are based on publicly available emission factor references and environmental research datasets. While values are approximations, they reflect realistic and scientifically accepted trends.

6. Who is the target audience of EcoRoute?

EcoRoute is designed for travelers, students, researchers, and environmentally conscious individuals who want to make informed and sustainable mobility decisions.

7. How does EcoRoute encourage behavioral change?

By providing measurable carbon impact data and sustainability scores, the platform makes environmental consequences visible. This transparency encourages users to choose greener alternatives and adopt responsible travel habits.

8. Can EcoRoute be expanded in the future?

Yes. Future enhancements may include real-time API integrations, AI-based route optimization, live pollution data, gamified eco-rewards, and database-driven user sustainability tracking.

9. How is this project relevant to climate action?

Transportation is a major contributor to global greenhouse gas emissions. By promoting low-carbon travel alternatives and spreading awareness, EcoRoute aligns with global sustainability and climate action goals.

10. Why was EcoRoute developed as a BTech project?

The project demonstrates how computer science and software engineering can address real-world environmental challenges. It combines programming, data analysis, and sustainability awareness into a practical, socially impactful solution.
